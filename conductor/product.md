# Initial Concept
A comprehensive suite of academic research workflow skills for AI coding agents (Claude Code, Pi, and Antigravity `agy` CLI), covering the full research-to-publication lifecycle with human-in-the-loop integrity gates.

# Product Definition

## Vision & Purpose
Academic Research Skills (ARS) augments researchers with a multi-agent AI copilot designed to eliminate repetitive academic grunt work while strictly preserving scholarly rigor and preventing AI failure modes. ARS does not write papers autonomously; rather, it enforces human-in-the-loop integrity gates, verifies factual claims against primary sources, and simulates rigorous peer review.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Academic researchers, PhD candidates, postdoctoral scholars, and research labs targeting top-tier peer-reviewed journals and conferences.
- **Secondary:** Independent researchers and technical investigators requiring verifiable provenance and rigorous evidence chains.

## Core Value Propositions
- **Human-in-the-Loop Integrity:** Stage-by-stage integrity checkpoints that intercept hallucinations, check citation validity, and gate progress on evidence.
- **Citation Anchoring & Claim Verification:** Three-layer citation anchors and automated L3 claim auditing against primary literature and Semantic Scholar APIs.
- **Quality & Authenticity:** Style calibration that learns author voice from prior publications, accompanied by checks that detect and eliminate synthetic, machine-generated prose patterns.

## Flagship Capabilities
1. **Multi-Agent Peer Review:** A 7-agent simulated peer review panel including specialized domain reviewers, a Devil's Advocate, and an R&R (Revise & Resubmit) traceability matrix.
2. **Citation Anchoring & Claim Auditing:** Semantic Scholar verification, 3-layer locator anchors, and gate refusal for fabricated or unsupported claims.
3. **Style Calibration & Writing Quality Checks:** Voice extraction from author baselines and detection of synthetic boilerplate phrases.
4. **10-Stage Pipeline Orchestration:** End-to-end management from research question inception to publication-ready artifacts with Material Passports and reproducibility locks.

## Strategic Roadmap & Evolution
- **Antigravity (`agy` CLI) Adaptation:** First-class adaptation and support for Google Antigravity CLI and agents, bridging Claude Code skill definitions and commands into the `agy` runtime, tool ecosystem, and multi-agent workflows.
- **Verification Hardening:** Ongoing refinement of claim-level audit passes, empirical boundary checks, and external experiment provenance intake.
