# ARS Paper Revision Workflow (ars-revision)

This workflow manages the revision lifecycle of academic papers, translating reviewer critiques and editorial decisions into targeted manuscript amendments and point-by-point response letters.

## Procedures

1. **Review Intake & Roadmap Alignment**:
   - Ingest reviewer reports, decision letters, and the previous manuscript draft.
   - Map each reviewer comment to the Revision Roadmap items.

2. **Targeted Draft Revision**:
   - Revise manuscript sections to address mandatory and recommended changes.
   - Preserve existing structure and citations while updating empirical analyses, clarifying discussions, or bolstering literature reviews.

3. **Point-by-Point Response Generation**:
   - Produce a formal Response to Reviewers document.
   - Quote each reviewer critique verbatim, detail the exact actions taken, and reference new line numbers and modified tables.
   - Ensure a respectful, scholarly, and non-defensive tone.

4. **Claim Drift & Verification Gate**:
   - Verify that all claims made in the response letter accurately correspond to actual textual modifications in the revised manuscript.
