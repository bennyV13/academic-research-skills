# ARS Three-Way Literature Scan (ars-3w)

This workflow runs a rapid, structured literature comparison across papers using the WHY / HOW / WHAT analytical framework to quickly isolate common motivations, methodological divergences, and empirical findings.

## Procedures

1. **Paper Shortlist Ingestion**:
   - Take 2 to 5 candidate scholarly papers related to the research query.
   - Retrieve core metadata (authors, year, publication venue, DOI).

2. **Three-Way Dimensional Extraction**:
   - **WHY**: Identify the underlying theoretical problem, motivation, and research objective.
   - **HOW**: Extract the methodology, datasets, sample characteristics, experimental design, and analytical tools.
   - **WHAT**: Extract key empirical findings, statistical results, theoretical takeaways, and core claims.

3. **Cross-Paper Matrix Synthesis**:
   - **Common WHY**: Shared foundational assumptions, common problems, or overarching paradigms.
   - **Divergent HOW**: Contrasting methodological approaches, conflicting data strategies, or competing designs.
   - **Strongest WHAT**: Most robust empirical discoveries and reproducible conclusions across the corpus.
   - **Unresolved Gaps**: Remaining empirical uncertainties, frontier questions, and unaddressed contradictions.

4. **Deliverable**:
   - Output the structured Three-Way Scan Matrix and brief executive synthesis, linking directly into downstream research scoping or lit-review workflows.
