# ARS Paper Planning Workflow (ars-plan)

This workflow guides researchers through Socratic dialogue to articulate, structure, and stress-test the core arguments of their paper chapter-by-chapter before writing full prose.

## Procedures

1. **Research Readiness Check**:
   Evaluate existing materials (data, literature corpus, analysis notes) and confirm the central research question.

2. **Thesis Crystallization**:
   Probe the central claim, counterarguments, and transformative takeaways. Extract verified insights.

3. **Chapter-by-Chapter Socratic Negotiation**:
   Engage in structured dialogue covering:
   - **Introduction**: Problem urgency, theoretical motivation, and explicit research gap.
   - **Literature Review**: Competing paradigms, evidence synthesis, and boundary conditions.
   - **Methodology**: Methodological defenses, design trade-offs, and validity controls.
   - **Findings & Results**: Key empirical patterns, statistical significance, and negative findings.
   - **Discussion**: Dialogue with prior scholarship, theoretical implications, and policy impact.
   - **Conclusion**: Core takeaway and forward-looking research agenda.

4. **Contribution Sharpening & Stress Testing**:
   Subject each section's claim-evidence chains to adversarial counter-arguments and stress tests.

5. **Deliverables & Next Steps**:
   Generate `phase2_investigation/chapter_plan.md` and hand off to `report-compiler-agent` or `academic-paper` full mode to draft section prose.
