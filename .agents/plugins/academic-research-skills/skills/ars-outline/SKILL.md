# ARS Paper Outline Generator (ars-outline)

This workflow constructs a comprehensive, publication-ready academic paper outline mapping every section to empirical evidence, theoretical constructs, and target word count budgets.

## Procedures

1. **Target Venue & Structure Alignment**:
   Determine the target publication venue, required structural template (IMRaD, Theoretical, Literature Review, Case Study, Policy Brief), and target length.

2. **Section Hierarchy & Heading Design**:
   Establish a clear 3-level heading hierarchy for each chapter ensuring logical argumentation flow.

3. **Evidence & Citation Mapping**:
   For each section, assign:
   - Primary claims and sub-claims
   - Supporting citations from verified literature
   - Anticipated tables, figures, or data displays

4. **Word Budget Allocation**:
   Allocate word count targets across chapters (e.g. 15% Intro, 25% Lit Review, 15% Method, 25% Results, 15% Discussion, 5% Conclusion).

5. **Deliverables**:
   Produce `phase2_investigation/paper_outline.md` ready for drafting or review.
